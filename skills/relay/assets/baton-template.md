# Baton

The living handoff between relay legs. Every leg rewrites this before it ends.
The mission itself lives in mission.md and is never edited.

## Mission digest

One paragraph, in your own words, of what mission.md asks for. If you cannot
write this paragraph from the mission file, the relay has drifted: stop and say so.

## Done so far

Nothing yet. (List completed work with the exact file paths and commits that prove it.)

## In flight

Nothing yet. (What was started but not finished, and the precise next action on it.)

## Next steps, ranked

1. Read mission.md and plan the first leg of work.

## Failed approaches, do not retry

None yet. (Every dead end goes here with one line on why it failed. The next
leg trusts this list; an approach missing from here will be retried and the
tokens spent on it wasted twice.)

## Learnings and gotchas

None yet. (Non-obvious facts about this codebase or task that a fresh session
would rediscover the slow way.)

## Drift check

Before ending a leg, state in one paragraph how the ranked next steps above
serve the mission as written in mission.md. If you cannot draw that line
honestly, flag the drift here instead of papering over it.
